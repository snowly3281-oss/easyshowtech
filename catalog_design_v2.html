Steel vs Aluminum

Specification Steel 3×3 Aluminum 3×3 Difference
Frame Material Galvanized Steel Aluminum Alloy 6063 —
Frame Thickness 1.2mm 1.5mm +25%
Total Weight 28 kg 15 kg -46%

Best For Long-term events Frequent travel —
Price Range $180-220 $280-350 +55%

Selection Guide

Choose Steel if you need a tent for long-term installations, frequent reuse, or in windy conditions. The cost-effectiveness and durability make it ideal for permanent or semi-permanent setups.

Choose Aluminum if portability is priority, you travel frequently to different events, or you need quick setup times. The lightweight design reduces shipping costs and makes one-person setup possible.
